## Summary

First **open beta** candidate with **stronger, honest cryptography** aligned to documented intent — not a marketing rename. Outbound DMs default to **NIP-17 gift wrap (NIP-44)**; community room-key distribution and portable profile export move to **NIP-44**; public website copy stops overclaiming metadata privacy.

**Communities (CU-UX L1):** Interest (T0) and Team (T1) create on Quiet or private hosts with honesty gates; Manage can convert mode/hosting. Dual-profile L3 soaks are **optional**, not a beta blocker.

This build is for **desktop evaluators** and **demo recording**. Tor remains **optional and unverified**. **1:1 voice** is shipped; **video is not usable** in this beta. Independent crypto audit has **not** been performed.

## Honesty (public beta copy)

**Safe to say:**

- Message **bodies** encrypt on your device before they leave.
- New DMs use **NIP-17 gift wrap**; communities use a **sealed room key** (AES-GCM on kind 10105).
- Relays carry **ciphertext**, not plaintext chat.
- You choose relays; **Tor is optional**; portable profile export uses modern NIP-44.
- **Open beta** — implemented and gated in CI, not independently audited.

**Do not say:**

- “Impossible to intercept or monitor”
- “No metadata anywhere” or “We don't track who you talk to”
- “Tor verified” or “Anonymous by default”
- “100% secure” or “Scam-proof”

Full sheet: [obscur-v2-known-limitations.md](../program/obscur-v2-known-limitations.md).
